BIOGRAPHY & SCHOLARSHIP

Bridging Sacred Philosophy & Critical Hermeneutics

Academic Background

I am an academic researcher in the field of Comparative Study of Religions and Sikh Philosophy. Having qualified the UGC-NET & JRF (June 2024), my work investigates how sacred texts codify metaphysical realities through symbolic language, historical context, and numerical paradigms.

Holding a Master's degree in Religious Studies with First Division with Distinction from Punjabi University, Patiala, and a Bachelor's Honors degree in Sri Guru Granth Sahib Studies with First Division with Distinction from Akal University, my research bridges classical textual hermeneutics with contemporary philosophical enquiry.
